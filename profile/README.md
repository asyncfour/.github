# Async4 🚀

### Four minds. One team. Different schedules.

Welcome to **Async4**, a student development team formed as part of the
**Trabajo en equipo en la Red** course at the
[Universitat Oberta de Catalunya (UOC)](https://www.uoc.edu/).

We're four developers and learners working together to turn ideas into
working software, one commit at a time.

## 🧑‍💻 What we're about

- 🤝 **Collaboration first** — Sharing knowledge and learning from each other.
- 🌍 **Async by design** — Different schedules, one shared goal.
- 🔄 **Agile mindset** — Planning, iterating, and continuously improving.
- 🛠️ **Learning by doing** — Building real solutions through teamwork.

## 📦 Our projects

This organization hosts the repositories, code, and technical resources
developed throughout our coursework.
